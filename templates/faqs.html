Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

How do I find schemes I'm eligible for?
You can browse schemes by category or use the search function. Each scheme listing includes eligibility criteria to help you determine if you qualify.
What documents are typically required for scheme applications?
Common documents include:
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Income Certificate
  • Residence Proof
  • Bank Account Details
  • Category Certificate (SC/ST/OBC) if applicable

Application Process

How can I track my application status?
Once logged in, you can track your applications through the "My Applications" section in your dashboard.
What should I do if my application is rejected?
If your application is rejected, you can:
  • Review the rejection reason provided
  • Ensure all documents are correct and up-to-date
  • Contact the scheme's helpdesk for clarification
  • Reapply after addressing the issues

Scheme Categories

What types of schemes are available?
We offer schemes in various categories:
  • Healthcare and Medical Support
  • Education and Scholarships
  • Housing and Infrastructure
  • Agriculture and Rural Development
  • Employment and Skill Development
  • Family Welfare Programs
