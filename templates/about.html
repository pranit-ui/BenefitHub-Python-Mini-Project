About BenefitHub

Our Mission

BenefitHub aims to bridge the gap between citizens and government welfare schemes through an intelligent, user-friendly platform that simplifies the discovery and application process for government benefits.

The Challenge

Government-to-Citizen (G2C) service delivery is crucial in ensuring that government welfare schemes reach the intended beneficiaries efficiently. However, citizens often struggle to find and apply for schemes suited to their specific needs due to fragmented information and complex eligibility criteria.

Our Solution

Our platform allows users to:

  • Access a comprehensive database of government schemes
  • Receive personalized scheme recommendations based on eligibility
  • Apply directly through the portal, reducing bureaucratic hurdles
  • Track application status and receive updates
