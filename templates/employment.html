Employment & Skill Development Schemes

PM Rozgar Yojana

Employment opportunities for youth with subsidized training and placement assistance.

Skill India Mission

Comprehensive skill development program for enhancing employability.

Startup India

Support for entrepreneurs with funding, mentorship and regulatory assistance.

National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme

Promotes apprenticeship training and provides stipend support to apprentices.

Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY)

Enables youth to take up industry-relevant skill training that will help them in securing better livelihood.

Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana (DDU-GKY)

Focuses on rural youth between the ages of 15 and 35 years from poor families.
